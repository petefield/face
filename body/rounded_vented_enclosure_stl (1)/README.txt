Rounded ventilated enclosure
============================
External assembled size: 120 x 120 x 95 mm
Wall thickness: 4 mm
Main body height: 87 mm
Top panel: 4 mm
Bottom panel: 4 mm
Outer corner radius: 8 mm
Vent holes: 6 mm diameter, perforated on all four side walls
Fasteners: M2
Panel clearance holes: 2.2 mm
Body screw-post pilot holes: 1.65 mm (intended for M2 screws threading directly into printed plastic)
Screw-post centres: 8 mm from each outside edge

Files:
- enclosure_body.stl
- enclosure_top.stl
- enclosure_bottom.stl
- rounded_vented_enclosure.scad (editable source)

Notes:
- The top and bottom panels are identical apart from filename.
- Overall 95 mm height assumes both 4 mm panels are included in the stated overall height.
- If using brass heat-set inserts rather than threading M2 screws directly into plastic, enlarge the boss holes to match the insert manufacturer's dimensions.
